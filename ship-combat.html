SHIP COMBAT

The Sixth Crew Member

Your ship isn't transport. It's a character — with its own statline, its own threshold for damage, its own breach conditions waiting to go critical. Ship combat in Voidheart runs parallel to personal combat: same Duality Roll, same Signal & Static economy, escalated stakes.

01 / The Ship Sheet

Ship Anatomy

Every ship has the following stats. Most are determined by frame — the kind of vessel you're flying. Upgrades and modules can shift them.

◈ HULL POINTS (HP)

The ship's structural integrity. When all HP fill, the ship is wrecked — non-functional and possibly venting atmosphere.

⌬ ARMOR

Damage reduction. Subtract Armor from incoming damage before checking thresholds. Heavily armored ships shrug off small-arms fire entirely.

⊞ MINOR / MAJOR THRESHOLDS

Two damage values that determine how badly each incoming hit lands. Damage at or above the Major Threshold triggers a Breach Condition.

◇ BREACH SLOTS

How many active Breach Conditions the ship can sustain before it loses critical function. Once all slots are filled, every further breach forces an immediate Hull Failure check.

✺ SYSTEMS

The collective number of functional subsystems — sensors, comms, life support, weapons, etc. Breaches knock systems offline; repairs bring them back.

↗ THRUST & ARMAMENT

Thrust is your maneuver bonus on Vector rolls and Evasive Pattern. Armament is your default weapon damage when firing a hardpoint.

02 / Engagement

Range Bands in the Void

Ship-scale ranges use the same vocabulary as personal combat, but the actual distances are an order of magnitude larger. A "Close" engagement on the ground is across a room; a "Close" engagement in the void is ships within visual contact of each other.

Melee
Hull-to-hull. Boarding range. Point-defense systems and torpedo proximity fuses. Ramming becomes a viable, if very stupid, option.
Very Close
Tight engagement. Short-range weapons effective. Visual ID without sensors. Knife-fight maneuvering.
Close
The standard combat envelope. Most ship weapons are accurate. Targeting solutions resolve quickly.
Far
Long-range engagement. Only long-range weapons effective. Sensor-dependent targeting; ECM matters here.
Very Far
Tactical edge of detection. Capital-grade weapons only. Comms lag becomes noticeable. Most ships disengage rather than commit.
03 / Crew Roles

Combat Stations

During a ship engagement, each PC takes one of the five stations. The trait listed is the primary one used for that station's actions, though clever play can justify other traits at GM discretion.

Helm

Trait: Agility · Instinct

Piloting, evasion, attack runs. The helm officer drives the ship and triggers the Evasive Pattern and Full Burn actions.

Gunnery

Trait: Finesse · Instinct

Weapons control. Resolves all Attack Vector actions and manages point-defense responses to incoming ordnance.

Engineering

Trait: Knowledge · Strength

Power routing, damage control, repairs in motion. Engineering rolls clear Breach Conditions and reroute power between systems.

Comms / ECM

Trait: Knowledge · Presence

Sensors, jamming, hails. The comms officer locks targeting solutions, breaks enemy locks, and handles all non-violent interactions during a fight.

Command

Trait: Presence · Knowledge

Coordination. The captain reads the field, calls maneuvers, and grants tactical bonuses to other stations through Rally and decisive direction.

With smaller crews, one PC can run two stations — but they only get a full turn at one of them. The other rolls at disadvantage. Ships with onboard NPCs or specialized AI can cover unattended stations.

04 / The Damage Flow

Damage & Thresholds

When a ship takes damage, subtract Armor from the incoming value, then compare the remainder to the ship's Minor and Major Thresholds. Where it lands determines the severity.

BELOW MINOR

The hull holds. Mark 1 Hull Point. Energy weapons might scorch the plating; kinetic weapons might dent it. Nothing important breaks.

AT OR ABOVE MINOR

Mark 2 Hull Points. Roll a d12: on a 10 or higher, a non-critical system flickers offline for one round (lights, gravity, internal comms).

AT OR ABOVE MAJOR

Mark 3 Hull Points and roll on the Breach Conditions table. A critical system is hit; something important is now broken or actively failing.

"Hulls are designed to fail gracefully. Cargo first, then crew comfort, then weapons, then maneuvering, then life support.
A ship that's losing gracefully is still losing."
05 / When Things Break

Universal Breach Conditions

When damage at or above the Major Threshold triggers a breach, the GM either chooses or rolls on this list. Each condition fills one Breach Slot and applies its effect until cleared by an Engineering Roll.

◆ THRUSTERS COMPROMISED

Helm rolls are made at disadvantage. The ship can't take the Full Burn action until cleared.

◆ WEAPON SYSTEMS OFFLINE

Choose one weapon hardpoint — it's down. No Attack Vector with that weapon until cleared.

◆ LIFE SUPPORT CRITICAL

Atmosphere, gravity, or thermal regulation failing. Every crew member marks a Stress at the start of each round until repaired.

◆ SENSOR BLACKOUT

Comms and targeting at disadvantage. Gunnery can't fire on targets beyond Close range. Comms can't lock or break locks.

◆ JUMP DRIVE LOCKED

You cannot Emergency Jump or initiate any FTL travel. The drive needs a full Engineering rebuild, or a long-rest's worth of work in a friendly port.

◆ HULL INTEGRITY COMPROMISED

The next damage roll against the ship is treated as one threshold higher. Stacking breaches stack the increase.

◆ REACTOR UNSTABLE

At the start of each round, the GM rolls a d6. On a 1, the reactor surges and a random station's officer marks a Stress. On a 6, the surge clears the condition.

Clearing a Breach

Engineering can attempt to clear a Breach as a station action: roll Knowledge against a Difficulty of 12 (15 for severe conditions like Jump Drive Locked or Reactor Unstable). On a success, the condition clears at the end of the round. On a Static success, it clears but you mark a Stress as you take damage from arcing power, escaping plasma, or sheer effort.

06 / Action Menu

The Four Core Ship Actions

These are the universal actions available to any ship. Specialized modules and upgrades add more.

▸ ATTACK VECTOR

Station: Gunnery

Roll Finesse or Instinct against the target ship's Evasion. On a success, deal Armament damage from your chosen hardpoint. Spend a Signal to add your ship's Armament bonus a second time. Crit applies Vulnerable to one of the target's systems until end of next round.

▸ EVASIVE PATTERN

Station: Helm

Roll Agility + Thrust. Until your next turn, all incoming attacks against your ship suffer a penalty equal to your Thrust value. On a critical success, the penalty doubles and the next attack to miss your ship grants you a Signal.

▸ EMERGENCY JUMP

Station: Helm + Engineering (combined)

Roll the higher of Instinct or Knowledge against Difficulty 14 (or higher if pursued or damaged). On a success, the ship jumps to a random adjacent system, ending the engagement. On a Static success, you escape but exit with a random Breach Condition. On a miss, the drive misfires — mark a Hull Point and you're still here.

▸ FULL BURN

Station: Helm

Push the engines hard. Roll Strength + Thrust against Difficulty 12. On success, move one range band closer to or farther from a target of your choice and gain +2 Thrust until your next turn. On Static success, you move but the engines overheat — the next Helm action is at disadvantage. Cannot be taken while Thrusters Compromised.

08 / Modifications

Ship Upgrades

Upgrades are major modifications that change how a ship performs in combat. Each can only be installed once. Acquiring them is a major story beat — a downtime project, a payoff for a heist, a reward from a faction, or the salvage of a wreck.

◈ REINFORCED PLATING

+1 Armor and +1 to both Minor and Major Thresholds. Adds visible structural weight; the ship looks tougher because it is.

⌬ TACTICAL AI

A combat-trained AI partner. Once per engagement, take an extra ship action on any station — the AI handles it. Roll at +1 Proficiency.

◇ STEALTH FIELD

A cloaking system that bends sensors. Once per scene, become Cloaked — no Attack Vector can target your ship until you fire or take a major action. Fades after one round of firing.

✺ OVERCHARGED DRIVE

+1 Thrust permanently. Full Burn moves you two range bands instead of one. The drive runs hot — if you take a Reactor Unstable breach, its d6 surge result triggers on 1 or 2.

✺ EXPANDED HARDPOINTS

Add one additional weapon hardpoint to the ship. Increases your maximum simultaneous Attack Vector targets by one.

◈ FIELD MEDICAL BAY

A trauma-grade medical cluster. During a Short Rest aboard ship, the crew can clear an additional Hit Point. During a Long Rest, also clear a Stress.
