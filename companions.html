COMPANIONS & XENOFORMS

The Symbiote's Shapes · The Hunter's Bond

Two classes need their own bestiary. The Symbiote chooses a Xenoform category each time they transform. The Hunter (Beastbound) travels with a bonded xeno-companion that levels up alongside them. Both references live here.

01 / Symbiote Transformations

Xenoform Options

When a Symbiote uses their Xenoform class feature, they choose a creature category of their tier or lower from the list below. At the GM's discretion, you can describe yourself shifting into any alien creature that reasonably fits the category.

⌬ HOW EACH ENTRY READS

  • Creature Category: A name describing the role of creatures in that category, with example creatures.
  • Character Trait: A bonus you gain to a trait while transformed. Lost when the form drops.
  • Attack Rolls: The form's range, trait used, and damage dice. You use your own Proficiency.
  • Evasion: Bonus added to your Evasion while transformed.
  • Advantages: Verbs the form is especially suited to — you gain advantage on related action/reaction rolls.
  • Features: Unique abilities of each form.

Skitterer, Shadow-Lurker, Tunnel Wraith, etc.

Advantage on: deceive, locate, sneak
Agile: Your movement is silent, and you can spend a Signal to move up to Far range without rolling.
Fragile: When you take Major or greater damage, you drop out of Xenoform.

■ VOID DRIFTER

Agility +1Evasion +3Melee Agility d6 phy

Fragile: When you take Major or greater damage, you drop out of Xenoform.

■ DEEP CRAWLER

Tide-Eel, Pressure-Fish, Void-Octopus, etc.
Agility +1Evasion +2Melee Agility d4 phy
Advantage on: navigate, sneak, swim
Aquatic: You can breathe and move naturally in liquid environments, including volatile atmospheric oceans and pressurized fluid tanks.
Fragile: When you take Major or greater damage, you drop out of Xenoform.

■ PACK BOND

Hound-Form, Companion-Strain, Bonded Grub, etc.
Instinct +1Evasion +2Melee Instinct d6 phy
Advantage on: climb, locate, protect
Companion: When you Help an Ally, you can roll a d8 as your advantage die.
Fragile: When you take Major or greater damage, you drop out of Xenoform.

■ VOID PACK

Snarl-Beast, Venom-Hyena, Rift-Wolf, etc.
Strength +2Evasion +1Melee Strength d8+2 phy
Advantage on: attack, sprint, track
Hobbling Strike: When you succeed on an attack against a target within Melee range, mark a Stress to make the target temporarily Vulnerable.
Pack Hunting: When you succeed on an attack against the same target as an ally who acts immediately before you, add a d8 to your damage roll.

■ CARAPACE HUNTER

Void-Tarantula, Fang-Spider, Lattice-Crawler, etc.
Finesse +1Evasion +2Melee Finesse d6+1 phy
Advantage on: attack, climb, sneak
Venomous Bite: When you succeed on an attack against a target within Melee range, the target becomes temporarily Poisoned. A Poisoned creature takes 1d10 direct physical damage each time they act.
Webslinger: Create a strong adhesive filament useful for traversal and battle. You can temporarily Restrain a target within Close range by succeeding on a Finesse Roll against them.

02 / Beastbound Hunter

Hunter Companion Sheet

When you choose the Beastbound Hunter subclass, take a companion sheet. This sheet tracks important information about your character's xeno-companion and can be tucked beneath the right side of your character sheet for ease of viewing.

Setup Steps

STEP 1 — Name Your Companion

Work with the GM to decide what kind of xeno-creature you have as your companion. Give them a name and add a picture or description of them to the companion sheet.

STEP 2 — Write Their Evasion

Fill in their Evasion, which starts at 10.

STEP 3 — Choose Their Experiences

Create two Experiences for your companion based on their training and the history you have together. Start with +2 in both Experiences. Whenever you gain a new Experience, your companion also gains one. All new Experiences start at +2.

Example Companion Experiences: Bold Distraction, Combat-Trained, Expert Climber, Fetch, Ferocious, Guardian of the Crew, Horrifying, Intimidating, Loyal Until the End, Navigation, Nimble, Nobody Left Behind, On High Alert, Protective, Scout, Sensor-Keen, Trusted Mount, Vigilant, We Always Find Them, You Can't Hit What You Can't Find

STEP 4 — Choose Their Attack & Damage Die

Describe your companion's method of dealing damage (their standard attack) and record it. At level 1, your companion's damage die is a d6 and their range is Melee. Choose whether they deal physical or energy damage.

Working With Your Companion

◈ CHANNEL ROLLS, SIGNAL & EXPERIENCES

Make a Channel Roll to connect with your companion and command them to take action. Spend a Signal to add an applicable Companion Experience to the roll. On a success with Signal, if your next action builds on their success, you gain advantage on the roll.

⌬ ATTACKING WITH YOUR COMPANION

When you command your companion to attack, they gain any benefits that would normally only apply to you (such as the effects of Hunter's Focus). On a success, their damage roll uses your Proficiency and their damage die.

✺ TAKING DAMAGE AS STRESS

When your companion would take any amount of damage, they mark a Stress. When they mark their last Stress, they drop out of the scene (fleeing, going to ground, etc.). They remain unavailable until the start of your next long rest, where they return with 1 Stress cleared.

When you choose a downtime move that clears Stress on yourself, your companion clears an equal number of Stress.

✺ LEVELING UP YOUR COMPANION

When your character levels up, choose one available option for your companion from the list below and mark it on your sheet.

Level-Up Options

Each option can be taken once. The Beastbound subclass features (Expert Training, Advanced Training) grant additional picks beyond the per-level default.

Intelligent

Your companion gains a permanent +1 bonus to a Companion Experience of your choice.

Light in the Dark

Use this as an additional Signal slot your character can mark.

Creature Comfort

Once per rest, when you take time during a quiet moment to give your companion attention and care, you can gain a Signal or you can both clear a Stress.

Armored

When your companion takes damage, you can mark one of your Armor Slots instead of marking one of their Stress.

Vicious

Increase your companion's damage die or range by one step (d6 → d8, Melee → Close, etc.).

Resilient

Your companion gains an additional Stress slot.

Bonded

When you mark your last Hit Point, your companion rushes to your side. Roll a number of d6s equal to the unmarked Stress slots they have and mark them. If any roll a 6, your companion helps you up. Clear your last Hit Point and return to the scene.

Aware

Your companion gains a permanent +2 bonus to their Evasion.
