DOMAINS

Nine Schools of Expertise

Each class has access to two of the nine domains. At every level, you choose a Domain Card from those two decks. Cards include channeled effects, techniques, gear, and signature moves.

HOW DOMAINS WORK

At character creation you choose 2 Domain Cards of level 1 from your class's two domain decks. As you level up you gain access to higher-level cards from those same two decks. Cards represent everything from learned techniques to specialized gear. Each one has a Recall Cost and either an ability you can activate or a passive benefit that applies while the card is in your loadout. Your loadout holds up to 5 cards; the rest go in your vault. Browse the full card reference to pick your starting loadout.

Your two domains define what you can do. Your traits define how well you do it.

▾ Domain Cards at a Glance

What's on a Domain Card?

Every Domain Card has the same parts, from a level 1 starter to a level 10 mastery card. See the full domain card catalog for every card in each deck.

◈ NAME & LEVEL

Each card has a flavorful name (for example, Hard Light Barrier, Phantom Retreat, or Onslaught) and a level from 1 to 10. You can only take cards at or below your character level.

⌬ RECALL COST

The number of Stress you mark to move the card from your vault into your loadout outside of a rest. Swapping cards during a rest is free. Your loadout holds up to 5 cards.

◇ TYPE

Ability cards are techniques and features you use directly. Spell cards channel a specific effect with a roll. Grid Archive cards (Grid domain) bundle several routines in one personal code archive.

✺ EFFECT

The actual rules text. Damage rolls, trigger conditions, costs in Signal or Stress, ranges, and any narrative permissions the card grants.
