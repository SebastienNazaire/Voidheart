DOMAINS

Nine Schools of Expertise

Domains are the conceptual toolboxes your class draws from. Each class has access to two of the nine domains, and at every level you choose Domain Cards from those two decks to add to your loadout — spells, techniques, gear, and signature moves.

HOW DOMAINS WORK

At character creation you choose 2 Domain Cards of level 1 from your class's two domain decks. As you level up you gain access to higher-level cards from those same two decks. Cards represent everything from learned techniques to specialized gear — each one has a Recall Cost and either an ability you can activate or a passive benefit you gain just by having it loaded. Browse the full card reference to pick your starting loadout.

Your two domains define what you can do. Your traits define how well you do it.

IRON
was Blade
Combat · Resilience · Armor

"The hull holds, or the hull doesn't. Make sure it holds."

Iron is the domain of close-quarters violence, weapons handling, and physical durability. Iron cards lean toward direct martial application — reinforced strikes, defensive maneuvers, weapon specializations, armored stances — alongside techniques for absorbing punishment that would put down anyone less prepared. Iron answers the question can you keep going? with stubborn, hammered-flat certainty.

VECTOR
was Bone
Motion · Piloting · Evasion

"The shot was perfect. They just weren't there anymore."

Vector is the domain of trajectory — piloting, motion, jump navigation, hard maneuvers, and the precise read of where things are going before they arrive. Vector cards favor those who treat space as a problem of geometry: fighter pilots, jump-drive navigators, freerunners and parkour specialists, anyone whose survival depends on being somewhere other than where the enemy aimed.

SHADOW
was Midnight
Stealth · Infiltration · Concealment

"They never knew we were there. That's the point."

Shadow is the domain of staying unseen. Cloaking tech, sensor-baffling rigs, dead-zone navigation, infiltration techniques, the discipline of not being noticed in rooms full of people who are paid to notice. Shadow cards favor those who treat awareness as a resource to be denied — spies, infiltrators, ghost-step operatives, and the rare hunter who tracks through stations and corridors instead of wilderness.

GRID
was Codex
Hacking · Drones · Electronic Warfare

"Every system has an input. Every input is a doorway."

Grid is the domain of systems — networks, drones, electronic warfare, breach code, and the digital architecture that holds civilization together when the analog edges fray. Grid cards range from precise single-target intrusions to broadcast-scale signal jamming, from drone control rigs to weaponized AI subroutines. If a thing has a circuit, Grid can talk to it. If it has a vulnerability, Grid can find it.

CIPHER

Science · Medicine · Engineering

"Understand it, and you can rebuild it. Rebuild it, and you can change it."

Cipher is the domain of applied knowledge — biotech, medicine, hard engineering, the disciplined work of understanding how something is built and using that understanding to alter, repair, replicate, or improve it. Cipher cards favor field medics, ship engineers, xenobiologists, weapons techs, and the kind of polymath who can rewire a life support system with one hand while running diagnostics on a salvaged xeno-artifact with the other.

Tide cards favor ship captains, battlefield commanders, charismatic frontline leaders, performers who use the crowd as an instrument, and anyone whose effectiveness depends less on what they can do and more on what they can get the people around them to do.

▾ Domain Cards at a Glance

What's on a Domain Card?

Every Domain Card has the same anatomy — whether it's a level 1 starter or a level 10 mastery card. See the full domain card catalog for every card in each deck.

◈ NAME & LEVEL

Each card has a flavorful name (e.g., Hardlight Barrier, Sensor Wash, Bonded Weapon) and a level from 1 to 10. You can only take cards at or below your character level.

⌬ RECALL COST

A number representing how much mental bandwidth the card takes up in your loadout. Higher-cost cards are more powerful. Your total loadout capacity scales with level.

◇ TYPE

Ability cards have an action you spend a turn to perform. Spell cards channel a specific effect with a roll. Grid Archive cards (Grid domain) bundle several routines in one personal code archive.

✺ EFFECT

The actual rules text. Damage rolls, trigger conditions, costs in Signal or Stress, ranges, and any narrative permissions the card grants.
