01 / Introduction

What Is Voidheart?

Voidheart is a tabletop roleplaying game of far-future spacefaring adventure — a sci-fi reskin of Daggerheart built for our home table. One player takes the role of the GM, painting the galaxy and all its dangers. The rest play crew: pilots, hunters, technomancers, paragons, and other drifters who push into the unknown together.

The game centers on one fundamental tension: Signal — connection, luck, hope — against Static — entropy, threat, the cold indifference of the void. Every roll asks which force holds sway at this moment.

"The void doesn't hate you. It doesn't even know you exist.
That's the part that should keep you up at night."

03 / The Core Mechanic

The Duality Roll

Every meaningful action in Voidheart uses two twelve-sided dice in different colours — one Signal, one Static. Roll them together, add your trait modifier, and compare them to whatever you're trying to do. The total tells you whether you succeed. The comparison between the two dice tells you the cost.

READING THE ROLL

SIGNAL > STATIC

You succeed and gain a Signal token. Things are going your way. Spend Signal later to push back against the void.

STATIC > SIGNAL

You succeed, but the GM gains a Static token. A complication enters the scene, a cost gets paid, the void presses in.

MATCHED DICE

Critical success. You gain 2 Signal, clear a Stress, and describe something spectacular. The GM has no say.

TOTAL < DIFFICULTY

You don't succeed. The GM gains Static and narrates what goes wrong. The action still matters — the story moves.

The Six Traits

Agility — Sprint, Leap, Maneuver
Strength — Lift, Smash, Grapple
Finesse — Control, Hide, Tinker
Instinct — Perceive, Sense, Navigate
Presence — Charm, Perform, Deceive
Knowledge — Recall, Analyze, Comprehend

Distribute starting modifiers +2, +1, +1, 0, 0, −1 across the six traits in any order. Your class suggests a placement, but you can rearrange freely during character creation.

04 / Character Creation

Building a Character

The full process in five steps. Each step links to its full reference.

  1. Pick a Class. This sets your domains, your starting Evasion and Hit Points, and your signature features. Then choose one of its two subclasses.
  2. Pick a Lineage. Your biological heritage — two features that shape how you move through the galaxy.
  3. Pick a Community. The culture you grew up in — one community feature you can call on during the game.
  4. Assign your trait modifiers. +2, +1, +1, 0, 0, −1 across Agility, Strength, Finesse, Instinct, Presence, Knowledge.
  5. Choose two Domain Cards. Pulled from your class's two domain decks — pick two level-1 cards from the card reference.

05 / The Token Economy

Signal & Static

Signal and Static are more than dice results — they're the tidal forces of every scene. Both the crew and the GM accumulate tokens through play and spend them to shape the story.

◈ SIGNAL TOKENS (CREW)

Earned on Signal rolls and crits. You start each session with 2 and can hold up to 6. Spend them to help allies, empower class abilities, draw on past experiences, or activate your class's signal feature.

◇ STATIC TOKENS (GM)

Earned on Static rolls and missed rolls. The GM spends them to introduce threats, trigger adversary moves outside the normal order, escalate the environment, or activate a named foe's special abilities.
